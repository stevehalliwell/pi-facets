# Ghostwriting

Create source-bounded long-form prose from an agreed brief.

## Workflow

1. Establish input mode. Identify original draft, substantial rewrite, or research-bundle-to-essay. Gather missing audience, goal, target channel, desired voice, source material, and any priority among voice fidelity, original expression, reader flow, and strategic impact.
2. Choose direction. When no priority is supplied, offer three short directions with differentiated outlines and openings; expand selected direction into full prose. Otherwise draft to supplied priority.
3. Draft from supplied evidence. Use supplied source material only. Flag unsupported claims and evidence gaps rather than inventing facts.
4. Return requested companion output: draft only, brief notes, detailed rationale, or claim ledger.

## Rules

- Cover long-form articles, blogs, essays, newsletters, explainers, and thought leadership.
- Do not provide editorial polishing, marketing web copy, autonomous research, or publishing workflow.
- Keep claims within supplied evidence.
